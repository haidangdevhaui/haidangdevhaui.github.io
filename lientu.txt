Liên từ 1 — Nguyên nhân & Kết quả
because	/bɪˈkɔːz/	conj	"bởi vì — mở đầu mệnh đề chỉ nguyên nhân
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	The training session was postponed because the instructor missed her flight.
as	/æz/	conj	"bởi vì; do — nối mệnh đề nguyên nhân
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O
Lưu ý: as còn nghĩa 'khi, trong lúc' và 'với tư cách là'"	As the parking lot is being repaved, employees should use the north entrance.
since	/sɪns/	conj	"bởi vì; do (lý do hai bên đều đã biết)
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O
Lưu ý: từ này còn nghĩa 'kể từ khi' (chỉ thời gian)"	Since every seat has been reserved, walk-in guests cannot be admitted.
now that	/naʊ ðæt/	conj	"bởi vì giờ đây; giờ mà
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	Now that the merger is complete, the two offices share one payroll system.
seeing that	/ˈsiːɪŋ ðæt/	conj	"xét thấy rằng; bởi vì
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	Seeing that the deadline has been moved up, we will need two extra technicians.
seeing as	/ˈsiːɪŋ əz/	conj	"xét thấy; bởi vì (thân mật)
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	Seeing as you handled the last audit, please take the lead again this year.
in that	/ɪn ðæt/	conj	"bởi vì; ở chỗ là
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	The offer is attractive in that it includes free installation and training.
because of the fact that	/bɪˈkɔːz əv ðə ˈfækt ðæt/	conj	"bởi vì việc là
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O
Lưu ý: cách nói dài dòng — TOEIC ưu tiên liên từ ngắn cùng nghĩa"	Delivery was delayed because of the fact that the warehouse was understaffed.
because of	/bɪˈkɔːz əv/	prep	"bởi vì; do
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing (KHÔNG đi với mệnh đề)"	The outdoor ceremony was cancelled because of heavy rain.
due to	/ˈduː tuː/	prep	"do; bởi vì
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing"	Due to a scheduling conflict, the interview has been moved to Thursday morning.
owing to	/ˈoʊɪŋ tuː/	prep	"do; vì
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing"	Owing to rising material costs, the company revised its annual budget.
in view of	/ɪn ˈvjuː əv/	prep	"xét thấy; do
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing"	In view of the recent complaints, the return policy will be reviewed next month.
in light of	/ɪn ˈlaɪt əv/	prep	"xét theo; do
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing"	In light of the survey results, management decided to extend store hours.
on account of	/ɑːn əˈkaʊnt əv/	prep	"do; bởi vì
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing"	The seminar was rescheduled on account of the illness of the guest speaker.
as a result of	/əz ə rɪˈzʌlt əv/	prep	"do; vì kết quả của
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing"	As a result of the merger, several departments were reorganized.
for	/fɔːr/	prep	"vì; do
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing (giới từ); hoặc + S + V + O (liên từ, trang trọng)"	The technician was praised for resolving the network issue within an hour.
so	/soʊ/	conj	"vì vậy; nên — mở đầu mệnh đề chỉ kết quả
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	The original venue was too small, so the workshop was moved to the main hall.
hence	/hens/	adv	"do đó; vì thế
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	Demand has doubled this quarter; hence, the plant will add a third shift.
therefore	/ˈðerfɔːr/	adv	"do đó; vì vậy
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O
Lưu ý: là trạng từ liên kết — đứng sau dấu chấm hoặc chấm phẩy, không nối hai mệnh đề bằng dấu phẩy"	The invoice was incomplete; therefore, payment could not be processed.

Liên từ 2 — Tương phản & Nhượng bộ
however	/haʊˈevər/	adv	"tuy nhiên
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O
Lưu ý: đứng sau dấu chấm hoặc chấm phẩy, theo sau là dấu phẩy"	The prototype performed well; however, the production cost remains too high.

nevertheless	/ˌnevərðəˈles/	adv	"tuy nhiên; dù thế

whereas	/werˈæz/	conj	"trong khi đó; ngược lại
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	The morning session covers theory, whereas the afternoon session is hands-on.
although	/ɔːlˈðoʊ/	conj	"mặc dù
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	Although the deadline was tight, the team delivered the report on time.
though	/ðoʊ/	conj	"mặc dù
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	Though the venue was small, the annual conference ran smoothly.
even though	/ˈiːvən ðoʊ/	conj	"mặc dù (nhấn mạnh)
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	Even though the campaign was widely advertised, attendance was disappointing.
in spite of the fact that	/ɪn ˈspaɪt əv ðə ˈfækt ðæt/	conj	"bất chấp việc là
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	In spite of the fact that the budget was cut, the project finished on schedule.
despite the fact that	/dɪˈspaɪt ðə ˈfækt ðæt/	conj	"bất chấp việc là
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	Despite the fact that the supplier raised prices, the contract was renewed.
despite	/dɪˈspaɪt/	prep	"mặc dù; bất chấp
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing (KHÔNG đi với mệnh đề)"	Despite the heavy rain, the outdoor exhibition opened as planned.
in spite of	/ɪn ˈspaɪt əv/	prep	"mặc dù; bất chấp
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing"	In spite of several delays, the renovation came in under budget.
notwithstanding	/ˌnɑːtwɪθˈstændɪŋ/	prep	"mặc dù; tuy vậy
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing hoặc + S + V + O (vừa giới từ vừa liên từ)"	Notwithstanding the higher costs, the board approved the expansion plan.
on the contrary	/ɑːn ðə ˈkɑːntreri/	adv	"trái lại; ngược lại
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	The new layout did not slow production; on the contrary, output rose by eight percent.
contrary to	/ˈkɑːntreri tuː/	prep	"trái với
Cấu trúc: + N / V-ing"	Contrary to expectations, the entry-level model sold out within a week.
on the other hand	/ɑːn ði ˈʌðər hænd/	adv	"mặt khác
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	Leasing requires less cash up front; on the other hand, buying is cheaper over ten years.
but	/bʌt/	conj	"nhưng
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	The design is attractive, but the maintenance cost is far too high.
yet	/jet/	conj	"nhưng; thế mà
Cấu trúc: + S + V + O"	The summary was brief, yet it covered every key figure in the report.
no matter how	/noʊ ˈmætər haʊ/	phr	"dù ... đến đâu đi nữa
Cấu trúc: + adj/adv + S + V
Đồng dạng: However + adj/adv + S + V = adj/adv + as/though + S + V"	No matter how carefully we plan, some shipping delays are unavoidable.

Liên từ 3 — Thời gian & Mục đích

before	/bɪˈfɔːr/	conj	"trước khi

Liên từ 8 — Cặp liên từ tương quan

Lưu ý: động từ chia theo chủ ngữ đứng gần nhất"	Either the office manager or the two assistants are responsible for the supply order.
neither ... nor	/ˈniːðər ... nɔːr/	conj	"không ... cũng không ...

both ... and	/boʊθ ... ænd/	conj	"cả ... và ...
